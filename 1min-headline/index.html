  • 언론사 홈페이지와 뉴스 피드의 주요 기사 제목을 모읍니다(국내판 13곳, 글로벌판 15곳).

1 Minute Headlines · 1분 헤드라인

YouTube channels @1min-headlines · @1min-headline

YouTube channels that sum up the day's top news in a short vertical video (Shorts). Both channels are made the same way.

  • 1 Minute Headlines (@1min-headlines) — top world news every day, in English, from 15 international news outlets.
  • 1분 헤드라인 (@1min-headline) — top Korean news every morning, in Korean, from 13 Korean news outlets.

How it is made

  • We collect the main headlines from news outlets' front pages and news feeds (15 international outlets for the global edition, 13 Korean outlets for the Korean edition).
  • We pick events covered by several outlets and order them by how many outlets reported them — never by expected views or interest.
  • An AI writes a short script based only on those headlines and their summaries; only stories that pass a number check and a fact check are included.
  • The anchor and the voice in the videos are AI-generated and do not depict a real person. Videos are labeled as altered or synthetic content on YouTube.

1-Minute Headlines Uploader

An internal tool the channel operator uses to upload each day's video to the operator's own channels (the two above). It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
